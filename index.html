The quick answer

What is a Kakobuy spreadsheet?

A Kakobuy spreadsheet is a product-research list organized around shopping through Kakobuy. It may record product links, categories, prices, sizes and comparison notes. Its usefulness depends on whether those details are accurate and current. Use this page as a starting point for category browsing and careful product comparison.

A practical Kakobuy shopping guide

A useful list records more than a link.

Keep enough information to explain why a product belongs on your shortlist. Missing details are a reason to investigate, not to assume.

  1. Pick one categoryBegin with a specific need, such as an everyday sneaker or a lightweight jacket.
  2. Compare like with likeUse the same variant, measurement units and cost assumptions for each candidate.
  3. Recheck before orderingReview the live listing, applicable terms and current total rather than relying on an old saved row.
Suggested fields for your own product shortlist
FieldWhat to record
Product & sourceDescriptive name, original listing URL and the date you checked it.
Exact variantSelected size, color, style and any option-specific price difference.
MeasurementsRelevant dimensions and units; compare with something you already own.
Photos & materialsAvailable images, stated materials and unanswered questions.
Estimated totalItem price plus applicable delivery, service, shipping, tax and payment costs.
Order conditionsAvailability, return conditions and any restrictions that apply.

A low item price alone does not establish value. Shipping and other charges can change the comparison.

Before you browse

Straight answers.
Better decisions.

A useful guide should make its purpose, limitations and next steps clear.

Does this page provide a downloadable spreadsheet?

No. This page is a category directory and research guide. Its shopping links lead to CAFANS, where you can explore listings and build your own shortlist.

How should I compare shoes and clothing?

Compare the exact variant, measurements, materials, available product photos and return conditions. For shoes, check the sizing method and foot-length guidance; for clothing, compare garment measurements with an item you already own.

Is the listed product price the final cost?

Not necessarily. Depending on the transaction, the total may include domestic delivery, service fees, international shipping, taxes and payment charges. Confirm the applicable costs before ordering.

Are the brand illustrations proof of stock or authenticity?

No. The Nike, Louis Vuitton, Gucci and Hermès illustrations are editorial artwork. They do not establish product availability, authenticity, affiliation or endorsement.

What should I do when product information is missing?

Mark the field as unconfirmed and seek clarification before purchasing. Do not infer materials, fit, authenticity or delivery conditions from a title or a single image.

Where do the shopping links go?

General browsing links go to CAFANS. Category links go directly to the corresponding CAFANS category. This page does not process orders.
